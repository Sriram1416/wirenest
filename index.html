Basket Size Guide 📏

Here is a quick reference to help you visualize our 3D Dimensions in your home.

S

Comparable to a Standard Coffee Mug or a Desktop Pen Holder. Perfect for small vanities, makeup brushes, and keys.

M

Medium Basket (8" x 10")

Comparable to a Standard Hardcover Book or a Countertop Fruit Bowl. Great for hand towels, fruit, or organizing pantry snacks.

L

Large Basket (12" x 14")

Comparable to a Stacked Laundry Pile or a Mid-size Houseplant Pot. Ideal for blankets, dirty laundry, or large children's toys.
